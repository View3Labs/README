# View3Labs Smart Contracts

This folder contains draft contracts for the View3Labs ecosystem, published for transparency.

## ⚠️ Nothing is deployed
**No View3Labs contract is live on any chain.** Anything on-chain claiming to be $VIEW3 or the View3Labs Founder NFT is fake. The only official contract address will be posted in one channel of our Discord, by the team, after a full independent audit. The team will never DM you first.

---

- **FounderNFT.sol**  
  ERC-721 collection (963 supply: 9 Founders' Circle + 90 Architect + 264 Pioneer + 600 Genesis). Founder access, governance, and utility. Equal rewards across tiers.  

- **VIEW3Token.sol**  
  ERC-20 token ($VIEW3, 1B fixed supply). Utility and governance for the ecosystem.
