## 🚀 Project Basics

**What is View3Labs?**  
View3Labs is a Web3-native creative studio. We make hand-drawn animated IP, intelligent digital collectibles, and a regenerative ecosystem that funds real-world impact.  

**What makes View3Labs different?**  
A named team, real animation craft, and AI trained purely on our own work. Plus, **10% of the total $VIEW3 supply is written into the tokenomics for ecological and social causes** — guided by holder voting.

## 🎟️ Founder NFTs

**What are the 963 Founder NFTs?**  
Your stake in the whole studio. Holders get:  
- Lifetime access to every project we launch  
- An equal base share of $VIEW3, the same for every tier  
- A real vote on community and impact decisions  

**How do the tiers work?**  
- **Founders' Circle (9)** + **Architect (90)** — invited  
- **Pioneer (264)** — earned in the community, then minted at a discount  
- **Genesis (600)** — public mint at 0.18 ETH  

**Do higher tiers earn more?**  
No — tiers shape access, rewards are equal. Contribution is rewarded separately and is open to every tier: a Genesis holder who builds can earn more than anyone.

## 🔐 Safety & Trust

**Where do I find official links?**  
Always use our **Website**:  
👉 [Official Links](https://view3labs.com/links)  

**Is there a contract address yet?**  
**No.** No View3Labs contract is deployed. Anything on-chain claiming to be $VIEW3 or the Founder NFT is fake. The only official address will ever be posted in one Discord channel, by the team, after audit.  

**How do I avoid scams?**  
- The team will never DM you first  
- Never share your seed phrase or private keys  
- Only click links from official channels  
- Bookmark our website links page & ENS domain (`view3labs.eth`)
