## The studio

View3Labs is a creative studio first and a Web3 project second. We make hand-drawn worlds, characters and stories, and we use on-chain tools to bring the people who believe in our vision into the studio as members.

The studio is led by Jamie Burns ([@jamiebxrns](https://x.com/jamiebxrns)): fifteen-plus years building and scaling an independent film and animation studio, working with global brands and leading agencies. He is now completing a master's in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at Liverpool John Moores University, where he holds the Redmond Prize Award. The creative team is hand artists, story developers and AI engineers. View3Labs also holds the 2026 Bathgate Award with a development grant.

We do three things, and we do them with passion: original IP, creative AI story companions and regenerative impact. Together they are the foundation.

## The first title

The Founder NFT gets you into everything we make. The first thing we make is a hand-drawn sci-fi saga, and it begins with the egg.

8,888 intelligent NFTs, released to collectors first: super intelligent companions that develop alongside a 24-week serialised story. Each one is an evolving character, raised in an interactive app the studio is building alongside the title. The title grows beyond the mint: anime shorts for socials, a short film series now in development, and brand merch made by the studio. Every piece is drawn by our art team. Our AI is trained purely on our own work, so everything it makes is ethically ours.

**Drawn by hand. Raised by you.** Each companion hatches from the egg with its own species and personality, then grows with the chapters and the choices you make. It always tells you it is AI, it is built for adults, and your personal memories stay private to you.

The first title and its app run community beta tests before wide release, and the View3Labs community is part of them: early builds, story chapters and the iNFT experience, tested and shaped with holders first. Its name is held back while we trademark the brand. The title and its world are revealed at the Founder NFT mint, and founders get first access to the egg.

## Regenerative impact

**A share of every sale. For ever.**

10% of net revenue from merch, licensing, the premium series and royalties goes to environmental and social projects from day one, rising to 20% once the studio is profitable: biodiversity, climate, conservation, and causes the holders choose.

Mint proceeds fund production.

The pledge is reported quarterly, with every donation verified. Holders vote on partners. Every contribution named, shown and reported. Creativity shapes culture, and culture should pull in the right direction. Regenerative means value that flows outward as well as upward.
